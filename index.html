Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Picvero – Photo Editor handles information when you use the app, including its photo-editing features, Google AdMob advertising, and Firebase Analytics.

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Last updated: October 21, 2026
At a glance: Picvero is designed to process the photos you select locally on your device. Advertising and analytics services may separately collect device identifiers, app activity, approximate location, and diagnostic information as described below.

1. Overview

Picvero provides tools for editing and exporting photos. Its core photo editing features are designed to operate on your device, and you do not need to create an account to use those features.

Some supporting services require internet connectivity. In particular, Picvero uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve the experience.

2. Photos and Images

Picvero accesses photos or images that you choose to open, edit, capture, or save. Core editing operations are designed to process those images locally on your device rather than upload them to our own servers.

  • We do not sell your photos or edited images.
  • We do not intentionally send photo contents to AdMob or Firebase Analytics.
  • We do not use your photos to train our own AI models.
  • We do not provide your photos to advertisers for marketing purposes.

If you choose to share an exported image through another app or online service, that destination's privacy practices apply to the information you choose to share. Other online or cloud-based features, if added, must be disclosed separately before release.

3. Permissions

Picvero may request access needed for a feature you choose to use:

  • Photos / Media: to select, open, edit, or save images.
  • Camera: if you choose to capture a new photo.

Where supported, Picvero may use Android's system photo picker to let you select specific images without granting broad access to your library. You can review or change app permissions in your device settings.

4. Local Storage

Picvero may save projects, temporary files, thumbnails, editing preferences, masks, and exported images locally to support editing, autosave, project recovery, and exporting.

You can delete locally stored projects where the app supports deletion, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Picvero. Files exported to other folders or apps may need to be deleted separately.

5. Data We Collect or Process

Although Picvero's core photo editing is designed to take place locally, Google advertising and analytics SDKs may collect or receive the following types of information, depending on device settings, SDK configuration, applicable consent, and how you use the app:

  • App activity: first opens, sessions, screen views, feature interactions, and events such as successful image exports.
  • Device and app details: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and technical characteristics.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or installation identifiers, Android advertising ID when available and permitted, and other SDK-supported device or app identifiers.
  • Network and approximate location data: IP address, which may be used to infer a general region or country. We do not request precise GPS location for advertising or analytics.
  • Diagnostics: information relating to SDK and app performance, such as startup timing, errors, or other technical signals.
  • Ad activity: ad requests, impressions, clicks, and related information used to deliver, measure, and protect advertising.
  • Consent preferences: information relating to the privacy choices you make when a consent message is presented.

We do not intentionally include photo contents, photo filenames, local file paths, names, email addresses, or phone numbers in custom Firebase Analytics events. The information handled by third-party SDKs can differ from the information processed solely by the photo editor.

6. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and improve photo-editing functionality.
  • To understand app usage, engagement, and feature performance.
  • To measure app acquisition, retention, and the effectiveness of updates.
  • To display and measure ads, including personalized ads where allowed.
  • To detect fraud, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.
  • To meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

7. On-Device Processing and Smart Features

Some features may use on-device image-processing or machine-learning components for segmentation, background removal, blur, masking, enhancement, or similar editing tasks.

A system component or third-party on-device SDK may download model files or other supporting resources. Picvero's core local editing workflow is not intended to upload your selected photos to our own servers for these processing tasks.

8. Third-Party Services

Picvero uses the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): delivers advertisements and processes ad-related usage, identifiers, device, network, and diagnostic information.
  • Google Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics: measures app usage, engagement, events, and technical information to help us understand how Picvero performs.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable: displays privacy messages and helps manage advertising consent choices.

These providers may process information on their systems and, for ad delivery, may work with advertising partners subject to applicable settings and privacy requirements. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps and sites
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

9. Advertising

Picvero may display banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, identifiers, ad interactions, and technical information to serve ads, measure their performance, and help prevent invalid activity.

Where allowed by law and your choices, ads may be personalized. Where personalized advertising is not permitted or not selected, ads may be non-personalized or limited, or an ad may not be shown. Non-personalized advertising can still involve certain device or network data processing.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your device's Google advertising settings, where the feature is available. This is different from removing data already collected by a service.

10. Firebase Analytics

Picvero uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated app trends, including first opens, active users, sessions, engagement, retention, screens visited, and usage of supported editing or export features.

Firebase may use app-instance identifiers and app/device information to measure these activities. Analytics data helps us diagnose user experience issues and decide which parts of the app to improve.

We do not intentionally transmit the contents of photos or edited images as Analytics event parameters. Analytics data collection and advertising-related uses are subject to applicable choices and the app's implemented privacy settings.

11. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Picvero uses Google's consent-management tools to ask for advertising privacy choices, including personalized advertising choices. Available choices may depend on your country or region.

Where a privacy-options entry point is required, you should be able to revisit your advertising consent choices from within Picvero. If you cannot locate this option or wish to make a data-related request, contact us using the details below. Advertising consent and Firebase Analytics collection are separate controls and must be handled appropriately.

You may also manage Android app permissions and your advertising ID through your device settings. Changing preferences does not necessarily erase information processed previously.

12. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your photos or intentionally share their contents with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies. Google and relevant advertising partners may receive the advertising, analytics, device, and network data described in this Policy to provide their services.

The legal treatment of some advertising-related disclosures, including whether they count as a "sale" or "sharing" under a particular law, may depend on your region and the specific advertising setup. Where applicable, available choices and rights will be provided.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Photos and projects stored locally remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Picvero, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior.

Advertising and analytics information handled by Google is retained according to the relevant Google service settings, policies, and applicable legal obligations. Clearing Picvero's local data or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete data previously collected by those services.

For questions about data access, correction, deletion, or applicable privacy rights, email us at umairdev600@gmail.com . We will review your request and explain any relevant limitations.

14. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary access to your content by keeping core editing operations local. Google's Mobile Ads SDK documents encryption of the data it transmits. No application, service, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

15. Children's Privacy

Picvero is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information through the core editor. Advertising and analytics settings for younger users, where relevant, must comply with applicable laws and Google Play rules.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

16. International Data Processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising and analytics information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their terms, privacy safeguards, and applicable law.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy when Picvero's features, SDKs, data practices, or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
